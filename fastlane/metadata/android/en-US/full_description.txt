Manja is a free meal wheel and recipe book. Add the dishes you like, spin the wheel, and whatever you ate leaves the wheel until the round is over.

No account, no ads, no tracking: everything stays on your phone.

<b>Features</b>
• <b>Meal wheel</b>: spin to pick a dish. Eaten dishes leave the wheel until it refills: when everything was eaten, after a number of days, or by hand. Themes: Classic, Pizza, Burek, Sachertorte and Baklava.
• <b>Categories and filters</b>: spin only mains, desserts or your own groups, limit calories per portion or the total cooking time.
• <b>Recipes</b>: ingredients in metric or imperial units, headings, steps, portions, photos and favourites. Recipes can be used as ingredients in other recipes, like a dough or a sauce.
• <b>Import from a link</b>: paste a recipe page and Manja reads it (for sites that publish schema.org recipe data).
• <b>Nutrition</b>: per portion or per 100 g, including vitamins and minerals. Built-in USDA data works offline; USDA and Open Food Facts can be searched online.
• <b>Barcode scanner</b>: scan a package and get its nutrition values from Open Food Facts.
• <b>What can I cook?</b>: tell Manja what you have at home and it shows the dishes you can make.
• <b>History</b>: what you ate and when.
• <b>Cookbook PDF</b>: print or share your recipes in three layouts.
• <b>Backup and move</b>: export everything to one .manja file and import it on another device.
• <b>Languages</b>: English, German and Bosnian.
• Light and dark mode, unit converter, and a few family recipes to start with.

<b>Privacy</b>
Manja uses the internet only when you ask it to: looking up ingredients at USDA or Open Food Facts, and importing a recipe from a link. The camera is used only to scan barcodes and take recipe photos.

Nutrition data: USDA FoodData Central (public domain) and Open Food Facts (Open Database License).
